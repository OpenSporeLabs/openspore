## Evidence policy

- `OBSERVED`: a persisted decompilation operation, current-source operation, or directly observed read/write.
- `SUPPORTED`: repeated static layout, SDK-name/structure evidence, or a bounded claim corroborated across independent artifacts.
- `INFERRED`: role or ownership interpretation based on access shape and context.
- `UNKNOWN`: unresolved semantics, aliasing, lifecycle, runtime value, or ownership.
- Confidence is confidence in the bounded static claim, not confidence in unobserved original runtime behavior.
- Decompiler types, indirect calls, enum names, and parameter names remain evidence, not truth.
- Every material row below carries a path plus VA and/or offset, confidence, and provenance.
