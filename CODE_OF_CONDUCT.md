# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone.

Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas, not people. Follow the
legal and ethics rules in `CONTRIBUTING.md` — no proprietary assets, no
leaked code, no DRM circumvention, no malware.

Examples of behavior that violates this code:
- Harassment, discrimination, or threatening language.
- Uploading proprietary EA assets, leaked/trade-secret code, or malware.
- Ignoring the clean-room rule by copying EA source.

Instances may be reported to a maintainer. Contributors who violate this code
may be removed from the project. This policy follows the Contributor Covenant.
